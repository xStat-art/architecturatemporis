Log Intervention

Records a real TIA intervention on one object, the full methodological way: assess the object's current state, apply the AT_ImpactMatrix type's rule, and the resulting CI is calculated — not typed in by hand. That's what separates this from the TIA Live Simulator, which stays a quick "what if" sandbox with a plain CI slider.
